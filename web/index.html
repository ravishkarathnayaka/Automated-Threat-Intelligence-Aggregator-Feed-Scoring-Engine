Mathematical Scoring Formulation

The scoring engine balances source authority, multi-source corroboration, and freshness decay to produce an auditable 0–100 confidence score:

Score = min(100.0, (Base Weight * Cross-Source Multiplier * (0.95 ^ Days Old)) + Enrichment Boost)
1. Source Weights
• CISA KEV: 1.00 (Authoritative)
• AbuseIPDB: 0.85 (Crowd consensus)
• URLhaus: 0.85 (Malware links)
• SOC Analyst Manual: 0.90
• Community Feeds: 0.60

• 1 Feed: 1.00x
• 2 Feeds: 1.25x (+25% boost)
• 3 Feeds: 1.45x (+45% boost)
• 4+ Feeds: 1.60x (+60% boost)
